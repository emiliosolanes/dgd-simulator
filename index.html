About this model

Personal project by emilio.solanes. Simulated data. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the companies shown.

Six export containers for MSC Gülsün at Hutchison Ports ECT Delta, Rotterdam, from Thursday 15 to Tuesday 20 October 2026. Every declaration, request and feedback item is exchanged as a DCSA Dangerous Goods Declaration (DGD) 1.0 message. DGD 1.0 is still in development; this model follows the alpha snapshot of 6 November 2026.

  • Real: MSC Gülsün, MSC and Hutchison Ports ECT Delta; UN numbers, proper shipping names, IMDG classes and EmS codes; the DGD 1.0 endpoints, resource names and vocabularies (declaration status DRAFT, FINAL, WITHDRAWN; review status PENDING, ACCEPTED, REJECTED; severity ERROR, WARN, INFO; RFC 6901 property paths).
  • Computed, not scripted: shipper issue times and the gate stop are inputs. Everything else follows from rules: Haventrek relays in 2 min; MSC checks a revision in 20 min and reports an accepted FINAL revision to the Harbour Master 3 min later; a new UN number is a reclassification, so r3 goes to MSC's DG desk, which re-checks stowage and segregation (45 min by default); ECT Delta pulls GET /dgds per booking with issuedDateTimeMin set 10 min before its previous poll, so a revision still being relayed is never missed, and drops the repeats this creates by reference and revision; shippers pull feedback the same way with a 5 min overlap and drop repeats by feedback ID; ECT Delta checks a FINAL revision 8 min after pulling it; the gate releases a held box 12 min after both ECT Delta has accepted the correction and MSC has re-approved the booking. The What if panel changes the polling intervals, the correction time and the re-approval time and recalculates whether the box makes the cut-off.
  • Assumed: a DG cut-off at ECT Delta on Sunday 18:00; the rule timings above; a gate check that reads hazard placards; all times, volumes and weights; a weekly service, so a rolled box waits 7 days.
  • Party views: Shipper, MSC and ECT Delta show only what that party has received or sent by the clock time. The physical lane under the chain is the world as it is, not any party's knowledge. All is the ops floor view across every party.
  • Fictional: Veldmeer Chemicals, Oostkade Coatings, Kadeveld Energy, Zeelwijk Process, Akkerzand Agro, Haventrek Logistics and their Singapore consignees; all booking, request and declaration references, seal numbers and the voyage number. Emergency contacts use the reserved .example domain. Container numbers carry valid check digits and are made up.
  • Not modelled: stowage and segregation planning; the Document Exchange transfer of referenced documents; registration, authorization and security; non-API exchange (UC9); MSC Gülsün's own port call, which video 1 covers.
  • Known simplifications: the Harbour Master receives a direct push, as in a port without a community system, while in Rotterdam dangerous goods on board are reported through Portbase's Notification Dangerous Goods service; MSC's re-approval reaches the gate outside DGD (an updated load list) and is not drawn as a message; a misdeclared UN number can also bring a fee or a refusal by the terminal, which is not modelled; routine checks take a fixed 20 min; today most of this exchange runs on email, PDFs, portals and EDI, and this model shows how it would run on DGD 1.0.
  • Payloads: bodies carry the fields this story uses plus those the DGD 1.0 conformance content checks ask for (parties, seals, certificates, emergency contact); other optional properties are left out. They validate against the alpha OpenAPI schema. Bodies follow the generated OpenAPI wrappers (DGDeclarations, DGDFeedbacks, DGDRequests). DGD defines no sending-party headers, so sender and receiver come from submissionOriginator, forwardingEvents and the feedback originatingParty.
  • Colours: Requested purple, Declared blue, Feedback amber, Accepted green. Accepted is a feedback item whose review status is ACCEPTED; it never changes the declaration's own status, which only the shipper sets. Red marks an ERROR, a rejection or a held box.

Sources: DCSA DGD 1.0.0 alpha package (use cases, information model guide, technical appendix, examples, OpenAPI). Portbase Notification Dangerous Goods, ILT mandatory ship notifications, MSC Gülsün, Hutchison Ports ECT Delta.
